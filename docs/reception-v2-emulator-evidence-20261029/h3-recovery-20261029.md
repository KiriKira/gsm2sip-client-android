## One-hour stream standby observation — partial, blocked by method

Started 2026-10-29 15:25:27.975 UTC with the screen off and Android reporting `mForceIdle=true`, `mState=IDLE`. The AVD had both light and deep idle disabled before this test; they were temporarily enabled and later restored to their original disabled state. No ADB/UI interaction occurred during the observed interval. At the user's request, the real-time wait was stopped at 2026-10-29 16:08:49.185 UTC, after 43 minutes 21.210 seconds; this is short of the planned hour and is not a pass. The partial diagnostic segment sampled at 16:05:18.179 UTC is `h3-hour-partial-segment.json`. Its process-cumulative counters include 43 idle polls, 42 successful and one failed RTC poll, 19 WSS opens and closes, and no currently held wake lock. These counters are not interval-only deltas and cannot establish hour-long behavior. At the stop endpoint, Android still reported force-idle/deep IDLE, screen off, network connected, and charging. The original AVD idle configuration was then restored (`mLightEnabled=false`, `mDeepEnabled=false`, `mForceIdle=false`, state `ACTIVE`).

The one-hour duration cannot be made equivalent by advancing QEMU/emulator time: instruction-count time (`-icount`) is not a reliable KVM wall-clock accelerator, and changing guest clock/timer rates would alter Android monotonic timers and diverge from real-time server, network, and FCM behavior. Batterystats, deviceidle history, scheduler/alarm records, logcat, and server ping/pong timestamps can corroborate only the wall-clock period actually observed; they do not replace it. Because the 43-minute interval was stopped, idle settings were restored, and H3 was shut down, its time cannot be accumulated with a later run. Keep this as BLOCKED (method) with partial evidence; a valid one-hour result would require a fresh uninterrupted 60-minute H3 observation.
